# Account security review

Accounts may be temporarily restricted if suspicious login, device, password, or payment activity is detected. This restriction is a protective measure and does not indicate permanent closure. Customers are notified by email when a restriction is applied.

A security review typically takes 1–3 business days. During this period the customer cannot trade, deposit, or withdraw. Open positions are maintained and will not be forcibly closed during a standard security review unless mandated by a separate risk decision.

## Immediate actions for customers

Customers who suspect unauthorised access should reset their password immediately, enable two-factor authentication if not already active, and contact support to report the incident. Support will log the report and escalate to the Trust and Safety team.

Do not advise customers to share passwords, PINs, or one-time codes with support agents. Support agents will never ask for this information.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second layer of verification at login. Customers can enable 2FA via the account settings page. Supported methods include authenticator apps and SMS codes. Recovery codes should be stored securely.

## Password reset

Password resets are initiated from the login page. A reset link is sent to the registered email address. If the customer no longer has access to the registered email, they must contact support to initiate an identity verification before the email can be changed.
