# Support prioritisation

VIP and security-related cases should be prioritised ahead of general informational requests. Cases involving account restrictions, suspected unauthorised access, or locked accounts are treated as high-priority regardless of customer tier.

## Response time targets

| Priority | Target first response |
|---|---|
| Critical (P0) | Within 1 hour |
| High (P1) | Within 4 hours |
| Medium (P2) | Within 24 hours |
| Low (P3) | Within 72 hours |

Response time targets are measured from initial ticket submission during business hours. Target times are not guaranteed and may be affected by ticket volume.

## Escalation paths

Payments and withdrawal issues that exceed the standard 3-business-day window should be escalated to the payments operations queue. Security incidents should be escalated immediately to the trust and safety team. Trade disputes must be routed to the trading operations queue.

## Customer communication standards

All customer-facing responses must be clear, empathetic, and grounded in documented policy. Agents must not speculate on outcomes, promise resolutions, or provide timelines that have not been confirmed by the relevant operations team.

Support agents must not provide financial advice, predict market movements, or recommend specific trading strategies.
