# Trade finality

Executed trades are final and cannot be reversed unless there is a confirmed platform error or an approved operational adjustment authorised by the trading operations team.

A confirmed platform error means a documented technical fault on the platform side — such as incorrect pricing data, a system outage during execution, or a settlement calculation error — that is verified by the trading operations team after investigation.

## Trade dispute process

Customers who believe a trade was executed incorrectly due to a platform error may submit a dispute through the support channel. The dispute must be submitted within 24 hours of the trade execution. Late disputes will not be reviewed.

Support agents must not promise trade reversal, credit, or compensation during initial contact. All trade dispute outcomes are determined solely by the trading operations team after investigation.

## Market conditions

Platform pricing reflects live market data. Spreads, slippage, and execution delays during periods of high volatility or low liquidity are normal market conditions and are not grounds for trade reversal.

## Position management

Customers are responsible for managing their own open positions, including setting stop-loss orders. Forced liquidation may occur if margin requirements are not met and is applied according to the platform's margin call policy.
