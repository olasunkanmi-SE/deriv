# Withdrawals

Verified customers usually have withdrawals reviewed within 24 hours of submission. Delays may occur if enhanced checks are required, payment rails are unavailable, or the account is currently under review.

Customers should allow up to 3 business days before contacting support. Withdrawal processing times vary by payment method and may be longer during high-volume periods.

## Additional checks

Support agents must not guarantee payout timing unless the payment provider has confirmed processing. If a payment is held pending additional verification, the customer will be notified by email with instructions for completing the verification steps.

## Refunds and reversals

Deposits that cannot be credited to an account may be returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days, subject to payment provider processing times. Refunds are not available for executed trades.

## Payment methods

Supported withdrawal methods include bank transfer, credit card reversal, and approved e-wallets. Withdrawal methods are restricted to the same method used for the matching deposit where required by compliance policy.
